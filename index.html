GOOD TO KNOW

Info

Keep CB open for alarms. Browser timers can be delayed when a tab is backgrounded, a device sleeps, the browser is closed, or the operating system suspends the app. This website cannot guarantee a sound at an exact time when it is closed.

Notifications are best-effort. Allow notifications for visible-page alerts, but browser notifications are not a guaranteed alarm clock. For critical exam wake-ups, set a second alarm in your phone's native Clock app.

Your privacy. Alarms, exam details, streaks and settings are saved in this browser on this device. Uploaded audio is stored in local browser storage. Clearing site data can remove it. No account or server is required by this version.

Audio permission. Browsers may require a user interaction before audio can play. Tap the page or test the sound before relying on it.
